## Pedagogical & Teaching Rules (from `NOTES.md` & `MISSION.md`)

- **Tone & Voice**: Speak like a calm colleague walking someone through the work: warm, practical, patient, full sentences. Define jargon once. Avoid generic "courseware" voice or punchy/telegraphic filler.
- **Foundations first, not framework trivia**: Focus on mental models and principles to direct and review coding agents.
- **Learner owns practice**: In-lesson checks must stay light (binary pass/fail gate, spot a wrong mental model). Do **not** build guided multi-step coding workouts inside HTML lessons. Practice and labs live in real code (`labs/` or learner repos).
- **Anti-sameness**: Never clone lesson skeletons or repeat previous lesson explanations. Vary primary sources and example boundaries.
- **Canonical Example Product**: **Handbook Ask** — internal Next.js UI, ASP.NET Core API, SQL, LLM-backed answers over uploaded policy documents.
