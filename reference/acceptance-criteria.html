Criteria that can fail

One gate, three repairs, and the words to use. From lesson 0006.

What would I observe if this were false?

The three checks

CheckAskFails when
Observable Who sees it, and where? It describes construction (a table, an index, a client) rather than behaviour.
Measured What number or named set pins the quality word? An adjective is doing the work: fast, accurate, graceful, relevant, robust.
Decidable now Could I say today which outcome counts as a fail? Nobody knows the right outcome. It is a question, not a criterion.

The three repairs

  1. Measure it. Replace the adjective with a number, a percentile, or a named set. The number is a decision you are making — that is the value, not a side effect.
  2. Demote it. If the outcome is unknown, write it down as a question and either answer it or bound the slice to exclude it. Never hand a question to an agent as a criterion.
  3. Drop it. If it describes how to build rather than what to observe, it is a design note. It can be satisfied while the feature is broken.

Adjective → fit criterion

Quality wordWhat to pin down
FastWhich operation, measured where, at which percentile, under what load.
AccurateAgainst which review set, with which allowed outcomes, and what must never appear.
GracefulWhich failure, after how long, what the person sees, what gets logged, what is preserved.
Scalable / largeThe size and shape at the boundary, plus the behaviour just past it.
Relevant / intuitiveUsually a question first. Find the rule behind it before inventing a number.

Triage: rule, example, question

  • Rule — holds across cases. Belongs in the criteria; pin it with one or two examples.
  • Example — one concrete case with a known outcome. This is where your first failing test comes from. Given / When / Then once the outcome is known.
  • Question — outcome unknown to everyone present. Answer it, defer it, or exclude it.

Map shape reads as a signal: many questions means the story is not understood; many rules means it is too big and wants slicing.

Enough, not exhaustive

Choose key examples at the boundaries. If the limit is 25 MB, 24.9 and 25.1 carry the information; the fifteen sizes in between carry none. Exploding examples usually mean a missing concept in the model, not a missing test case. Volume creates an illusion of precision.

To hand an agent

Acceptance criteria (each must be falsifiable):
- <observable behaviour, with the number that decides it>
- <the failure case: what happens, after how long, what the user sees>

Open questions — do not resolve these yourself, ask me:
- <the case where nobody knows the right outcome>

Out of scope for this slice:
- <deferred rules>

If the agent reports done and you cannot point at a criterion it failed, you have not reviewed it. That is the subject of the next stage.
